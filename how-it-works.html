How it works

Right now, every order is reviewed by a person — not fully automated. That's deliberate.

1. Choose your package

Pick the check that fits your document type and approximate word count from our pricing page.

2. Tell us about your document

Submit the request form with your details. We reply with secure document-upload instructions and payment details — we don't take payment or documents through an unsecured form.

3. Send your document & pay

Once confirmed, you share your document through the secure link we send and complete payment.

4. We run the checks

Similarity analysis, AI-writing assessment, and citation/reference/DOI verification are run and reviewed by our team before anything is sent to you.

5. You get your report

A clear, downloadable report with problem sections highlighted and concrete next steps.

6. Fix and resubmit if needed

Packages with revisions included let you send an updated version for a follow-up check.

Why manual review, for now? We're validating demand and provider costs before building a fully automated pipeline. Manual review also means a person checks your report for accuracy before you see it — not just a raw score.

Ready to start?

Typical turnaround is communicated when we confirm your order. Need it faster? Ask about urgent processing.
