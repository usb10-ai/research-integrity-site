Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026. Straightforward rules, no fine-print surprises.

Before processing begins

You can cancel for a full refund any time before we've started reviewing your document — in practice, this means any time before we confirm your upload has been received and processing has started. Just email us to cancel.

After processing has started

Once we've begun running checks on your document, real costs are already incurred (similarity/AI-detection processing and manual quality review), so orders are non-refundable from that point, except where we fail to deliver as described below.

If we can't deliver

If we're unable to complete your check — for example, an unsupported document type or a technical failure on our end — you'll receive a full refund, no questions asked.

Revisions

The Journal Ready package includes 2 revisions (re-checking an updated version of the same document after you've made changes). Other packages don't include revisions by default, but an additional revision can be added to any order for ₹199.

Turnaround delays

If we significantly miss the turnaround time we confirmed with you at order time, and haven't communicated a delay in advance, you're entitled to request a partial refund reflecting the inconvenience — email us and we'll work it out fairly.

How to request a refund

Email work.researchintegrity@gmail.com with your order details. We aim to respond to refund requests within 2 business days.
