Privacy & Confidentiality

Last updated: August 2026. Your manuscript is yours — here's exactly how we handle it.

What we collect

When you submit a request, we collect the contact details you provide (name, email, phone, institution), the document(s) you share with us for review, and basic order records (package selected, add-ons, order date). If online payment is enabled in future, payment is handled by a third-party payment processor — we do not store your card or bank details ourselves.

How your document is handled

Your document is used solely to carry out the checks you ordered: similarity analysis, AI-writing assessment, citation/reference/DOI verification, and generating your report. We do not publish it, submit it anywhere on your behalf, sell or share it with unrelated third parties, or use it to train any AI/ML model.

Who can see it

Access is limited to the reviewers directly involved in processing your order. Automated checks may be run through third-party similarity/AI-detection tools as part of generating your report; those providers process your document only to return a result to us, under their own data-handling terms, and are not permitted to independently publish or reuse it.

How your document reaches us

We do not accept manuscripts through the public order form. After you submit a request, we follow up with a secure, individual link for you to upload your document, and confirm payment separately — this avoids sending sensitive files over email or storing them in an open form submission.

Retention & deletion

We keep your document and report only as long as needed to complete your order and handle any follow-up revisions, typically no longer than 90 days after delivery, after which it is deleted from our working storage. You can request earlier deletion at any time by emailing us, and we will confirm once it's done.

Third-party providers

We use Web3Forms to deliver order-request notifications to our inbox, and may use commercial similarity/AI-detection services to run checks. We only work with providers whose terms permit this kind of use, and we do not use providers that claim ownership or resale rights over the content you submit.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what data we hold about you, request a copy of it, ask us to correct inaccurate details, or request deletion of your document, report, and contact records (subject to any legal requirement to retain order/payment records for a limited period).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects how we handle your data, we'll update the "last updated" date above. Significant changes will be communicated to customers with active or recent orders.

Contact

Questions about your data can be sent to work.researchintegrity@gmail.com.
